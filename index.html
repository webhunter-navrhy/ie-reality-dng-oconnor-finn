Fiona Waldron of DNG O Connor Finn Auctioneers is proud to present this exceptional detached family residence extending to approximately c. 4,100 sq. ft., standing proudly on a beautifully maintained c. 2.6 acre site. Built in 2004 to an exceptionally high standard, this impressive home combines elegant design, generous accommodation and premium finishes throughout, while enjoying stunning panoramic countryside views. Nestled in the heart of the East Cork countryside at Ballymaloe Cross.

Approached through a welcoming entrance porch with solid wooden front door and glass side panels, the property immediately impresses with its bright and spacious interiors. The entrance hallway features luxurious white marble flooring and a striking turned staircase complete with runner carpet.

The main living room is a superb dual-aspect reception room featuring solid oak flooring, a solid fuel fireplace, recessed lighting and Roman blinds, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. The dining room overlooks the front gardens and benefits from French doors opening onto a side patio area, ideal for outdoor entertaining.

At the heart of the home lies the bespoke Glenline solid oak kitchen with black granite worktops and an impressive island unit offering extensive storage. A Rangemaster cooker with five-ring hob and overhead extractor fan completes this outstanding kitchen space. Just off the kitchen is a walk-in pantry and a second living room overlooking the exquisite gardens to the side and rear.

The property offers exceptional versatility with a large utility room, guest bathroom and an adjoining fully insulated garage area with independent access, making it ideal for conversion into a self-contained unit subject to requirements.

The accommodation is extensive and flexible, comprising five/seven spacious bedrooms in total, including a luxurious master suite with walk-in wardrobe, built-in dressing area and a beautifully appointed en-suite bathroom. Bathrooms throughout the property are finished to a high standard with fully tiled pumped showers, quality sanitary ware and chrome heated towel rails. All showers are Glass fibre tanking waterproofed showers.

The upper floors continue to impress with additional large bedrooms, Velux windows, polished pine flooring, built-in storage and stunning countryside views from multiple aspects.

Externally, the grounds are beautifully maintained and include a substantial 5m x 4m garden shed with full planning approval.

Hakabe Hill offers an exceptional standard of living in one of East Cork’s most sought-after countryside settings, within easy reach of Ballymaloe, Midleton and Cork City.
